# Dataset & Signal Chain

> Status: ✅ **Implemented** for the 4-band 10 m case. 🧪 Synthetic for training. ⌛ 12-band planned.

## Input signal

GeoSR ingests **Sentinel-2 MSI Level-2A** multispectral GeoTIFF imagery restricted to the
four 10 m bands:

| Band | Name | Center λ (nm) | Native GSD |
|---|---|---|---|
| B02 | Blue | 490 | 10 m |
| B03 | Green | 560 | 10 m |
| B04 | Red | 665 | 10 m |
| B08 | NIR | 842 | 10 m |

Pixel values are stored as **uint16 Digital Numbers (DN)**. GeoSR treats the DN as a proxy
for surface reflectance and normalizes with **`reflectance = DN / 10000`**, clipping to
`[0, 1.5]` — the Sentinel-2 TOA reflectance range used during training
(`REFLECTANCE_SCALE = 10000.0` in `model/inference.py`). The inverse transform is applied
on write so output GeoTIFFs remain in DN uint16, matching the source dtype.

## CRS & geotransform

- Bands are preserved in **B02, B03, B04, B08** order through ingest, tiling, inference,
  reconstruction, and export.
- Output CRS is inherited verbatim from the input; the affine transform is scaled by
  `1/scale_factor` (pixel size 10 m → 2.5 m) with the **same top-left origin**, so the
  output bounds coincide with the input bounds.
- `GeoTIFFWriter.verify_geospatial_alignment` asserts CRS match, `out.shape == in.shape * s`,
  resolution ratio, and bound coincidence within `1e-4`.

## Training data

Training pairs are **synthetic**, generated by `model/datasets/degradation.py` from
synthetic 2.5 m/10 m "HR" reflectance scenes. The forward operator is a controlled
approximation:

1. bicubic downscale to the LR resolution,
2. additive Gaussian noise (per `DegradationConfig`),
3. optional blur.

The same `degrade` operator is used for the round-trip self-consistency check, so the
round-trip residual is a **consistent** (if approximate) signal.

> ⚠️ **Training-vs-deployment resolution gap (CRITICAL).** The model is trained on
> **40 m → 10 m** pairs but is deployed on **10 m → 2.5 m**. Both are 4×, so the *network
> upscaling operator* is exercised in-distribution, but the **absolute spatial frequency**
> of the target is out-of-distribution: 2.5 m detail cannot be recovered from 10 m input
> beyond what the input constrains. The legacy ESGAN precompute numbers for 10 m→2.5 m are an
> honest measure of this **extrapolation regime**, not true 2.5 m information recovery.
> This is documented in `model/datasets/DATASET.md` and surfaced in the legacy ESGAN precompute artifacts (`data/demo/{urban,agriculture,water}/`); the live `/demo` uses GeoSRv2 (10 m→5 m, ×2).

## Splits

`model/datasets/splits.py` defines `BAND_NAMES_10M` and a deterministic 80/10/10
train/val/test split with a fixed seed (42), guaranteeing no scene leakage between
training and validation.

## Limitations / caveats

- Only 4 bands are processed end-to-end. ⌛ Full 12-band (SWIR, visible) is planned.
- The degradation model is a **training approximation**, not a physical sensor simulator.
- Synthetic reference scenes are **not real satellite imagery**.
